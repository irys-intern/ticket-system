# Getting Started

Welcome to the support team! This guide covers the basics of handling tickets.

## Your First Day

- Review open tickets in your queue
- Familiarize yourself with the ticket statuses: **Open**, **In Progress**, **Waiting for Response**, **Resolved**, **Closed**
- Reach out to your team lead if you have questions

## Claiming a Ticket

1. Navigate to **Open Tickets**
2. Find an unassigned ticket relevant to your expertise
3. Click **Claim** to assign it to yourself
4. Update the status to **In Progress**

## Responding to Users

Always acknowledge the user's issue before asking clarifying questions. Be clear, concise, and empathetic.
