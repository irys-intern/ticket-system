# Priority Levels

Priority reflects how urgently a ticket needs to be resolved. Set it based on impact and time-sensitivity, not on how frustrated the user sounds. Note that this may differ from what is requested by the user.

## Low

Something is inconvenient but has a workaround or no immediate consequence. Examples: cosmetic issues, minor documentation gaps, questions with no deadline.

**Target first response:** within 2 business days.

## Medium

A real problem that is affecting the user's work but not blocking it entirely. Most tickets fall here. Examples: a feature behaving unexpectedly, slow performance, confusion about how something works.

**Target first response:** within 1 business day.

## High

The user is significantly blocked or a key workflow is broken. Examples: a core feature is unavailable, a workaround exists but is seriously burdensome.

**Target first response:** within 4 hours.

## Critical

A complete blocker with no workaround, or a situation with potential data loss or security implications. Examples: service is down, data is inaccessible, credentials are compromised.

**Target first response:** within 1 hour. Notify your team lead immediately.

---

**Note:** If you disagree with the priority a user set when submitting their ticket, update it and leave a comment explaining why. Do not silently change it.
